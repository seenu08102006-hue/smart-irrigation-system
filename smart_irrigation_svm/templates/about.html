About the project

Farms often irrigate on a fixed timer, which wastes both water and pump electricity. This project predicts whether irrigation is really needed and only then switches the pump on.

How it works
  1. Sensors give temperature, humidity, soil moisture and rainfall.
  2. A trained Support Vector Machine classifies the situation as Irrigation Required / Not Required.
  3. The electrical optimisation module converts the decision into pump ON/OFF, litres, run-time and kWh.
  4. The kWh is compared with a fixed 2-hour daily timer to show the savings.
Technology

Python · Pandas · NumPy · Scikit-learn · Matplotlib · Joblib · Flask · Bootstrap 5

Note: the training data is synthetic (generated from agronomy rules). Pump power, flow rate and the timer baseline are assumptions defined in src/config.py.
