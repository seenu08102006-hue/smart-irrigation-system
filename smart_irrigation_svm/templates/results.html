Why SVM?
  • Maximum-margin boundary - generalises well on noisy sensor data.
  • RBF kernel - captures the non-linear effect of heat, humidity and rain without hand-made features.
  • Compact model - only the support vectors matter, so prediction is fast enough for an embedded controller.
  • Top ROC-AUC / F1 in our tests, with recall high enough that thirsty crops are rarely missed.
